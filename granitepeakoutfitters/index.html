Google UX Certificate Project

Granite Peak Outfitters

A fictional camping supply store designed to provide an easy, organized, and enjoyable shopping experience for outdoor enthusiasts and first-time campers. This project is being developed as part of the Google UX Design Professional Certificate, and the case study will continue to be updated as I progress through the course.

Project Overview

Granite Peak Outfitters is a fictional e-commerce project centered on creating a clear and supportive shopping experience for campers of varying experience levels. The design organizes products, specifications, comparisons, and educational resources so users can evaluate their options and choose appropriate camping gear with confidence.

Low-Fidelity Desktop Wireframe

Designing the Desktop Shopping Experience

The lo-fi wireframes establish the structure and flow of the Granite Peak Outfitters shopping experience before visual styling is introduced. The layouts focus on clear navigation, organized product categories, helpful camping resources, and a straightforward path from product discovery to checkout.
