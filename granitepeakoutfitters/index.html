Google UX Certificate Project

Granite Peak Outfitters

A fictional camping supply store designed to provide an easy, organized, and enjoyable shopping experience for outdoor enthusiasts and first-time campers. This project is being developed as part of the Google UX Design Professional Certificate, and the case study will continue to be updated as I progress through the course.

Project Overview

Granite Peak Outfitters is a fictional e-commerce project designed to create a clear, supportive shopping experience that helps campers find, compare, and choose the right gear with confidence.

The Problem

Outdoor gear websites often overwhelm users with too many options, complex product information, and a lack of guidance for first-time campers.

The Goal

Design an approachable and efficient shopping experience that helps users easily discover, compare, and purchase the right gear for their adventures.

Understanding the Users

To help define the needs of Granite Peak Outfitters' target users, I used AI-assisted persona development to explore two representative user types: a first-time family camper and an experienced solo hiker. These personas helped guide decisions around navigation, product information, filtering, comparison, and beginner-friendly guidance.

Key User Pain Points

  • Navigation Large catalogs make products difficult to locate.
  • Product Information Technical specs can overwhelm inexperienced shoppers.
  • Comparison Experienced shoppers need an easy way to compare products.
  • Beginner Guidance New campers may not know which equipment they need.
