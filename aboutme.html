About

I’m Joe Fugere.

A web designer and front-end developer focused on building clean, intentional digital experiences.

Introduction

Design, built with clarity and intention.

I design and build websites with a focus on clarity, structure, and usability. Every decision—from layout and typography to spacing and interaction—is made with intention.

My goal is simple: create digital experiences that feel clean, modern, and effortless to use.

Background

Built through real projects and hands-on work.

My path into web design has been shaped through hands-on experience, building projects that combine design, front-end development, branding, and real-world problem solving.

Through building J | F Candle Co., I’ve gained practical experience refining a live e-commerce brand, improving website sections, creating product content, developing brand visuals, and thinking through the full customer experience.

I’m also developing concept projects like Ritual Supply Co. to strengthen my portfolio, practice modern layouts, and show how I approach design, structure, and front-end development.

Currently

Continuing to refine and expand.

I’m currently focused on strengthening my front-end development skills, expanding into JavaScript to build more dynamic experiences, and continuing to develop thoughtful, well-structured case studies.
